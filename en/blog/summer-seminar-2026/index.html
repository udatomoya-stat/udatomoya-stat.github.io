Presentation at Statistics Summer Seminar 2026

August 5, 2026 · Presentation

I gave a presentation titled “Spatio-temporal Coarse-Grained Hawkes Processes” at Statistics Summer Seminar 2026. The presentation was delivered in Japanese.

Presentation Information

  • Presenter: Tomoya Uda
  • Title: Spatio-temporal Coarse-Grained Hawkes Processes
  • Event: Statistics Summer Seminar 2026 (website)
  • Date: August 5, 2026
  • Language: Japanese

Overview

Building on the temporally coarse-grained Hawkes process (Koyama, 2025), this work extends the framework from temporal to spatio-temporal settings. In particular, rather than assuming a point mass for event locations within each spatial cell, as in the conventional method, the proposed method allows for a spatially spread distribution. This makes the stationary mean consistent with that of the binned Hawkes process. The method also achieves a higher order of accuracy for the stationary covariance than the conventional method. Extending the framework to spatio-temporal settings also broadens its potential applications to real-world data. In an application to earthquake data, the method achieved predictive performance comparable to machine-learning-based methods even though it did not use the complete dataset. Because the method uses count data, it also offers computational advantages.

Reflections

This was my first presentation, and I was pleased to receive several questions. Each of them provided useful guidance for future work. In particular, I would like to investigate whether the process exists mathematically and how its explicit temporal evolution should be formulated.
