Presentation at the Japanese Joint Statistical Meeting 2026

September 10, 2026 · Presentation

I gave a presentation titled “Spatio-temporal Coarse-Grained Hawkes Processes” at the Japanese Joint Statistical Meeting 2026. The presentation was delivered in Japanese.

Presentation Details

  • Presenter: Tomoya Uda
  • Co-author: Shinsuke Koyama
  • Title: Spatio-temporal Coarse-Grained Hawkes Processes
  • Event: Japanese Joint Statistical Meeting 2026
  • Date: September 10, 2026

Overview

The content was essentially the same as that of my presentation at the Statistics Summer Seminar, but I shortened the introduction and placed greater emphasis on the mathematical explanation. In particular, I included a concrete method for describing the temporal evolution of counts that I developed after the previous presentation.

Reflections

This was my first presentation at an academic conference, and I was nervous. I would like to thank the faculty members and students who attended. The conference as a whole, including its networking events, also inspired me to hope that we can gradually expand the point process community within the institute. One lesson I took away was that I needed more time to prepare my slides and rehearse the presentation.
